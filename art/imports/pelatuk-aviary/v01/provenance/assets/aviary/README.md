# Original Aviary asset sources

Project-authored assets for the realism harness, 12-13 November 2026. Owner requests original editable digital assets and prioritizes quality over duration. These drafts are not integrated into the production renderer.

## Bird meshes and articulation

Editable parametric source: `src/aviary/asset-lab/woodpecker/`. Original authored continuous body loft, sculpted head, chisel bill/mandible, curved feather blades, contour tips, feet, articulated groups and species pigmentation. `tests/aviary/visual-lab.html` previews both species and exports GLB geometry/materials/animation tracks, then reloads the actual GLB for inspection. No downloaded bird mesh, stock texture or reference photo is incorporated. Species identification references inform anatomy and coloration only; they do not license copying source photos.

Draft GLBs and their container/resource inspection are in `exports/`. V01/v02 contain four embedded material images, v03 contains six. Each has eight two-second study clips: perch, drum, walk, hop, fly, eat, drink, rest. These clips remain quality studies; takeoff/landing, loop continuity and final lifelike motion are not accepted. Articulation uses editable node transforms; v09 adds a native two-joint cervical skin on the continuous torso. Native source controls the mesh construction and authored poses; GLB is an interoperable snapshot.

GLB root transforms calibrate the nominal bill-to-tail straight-line span in the canonical perch pose to 0.13 m and 0.29 m respectively. The calibration and scientific name are stored in root extras. This is an authored scale convention based on species size references, not a certified anatomical measurement. The harness compensates the display scale to inspect either species closely at the same camera framing.
