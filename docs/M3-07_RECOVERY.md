## Player outcome and rules

The EN/ID recovery card gives one actual next step, its cost or refund, and a review/Confirm/Cancel dialog. It appears below100 ZC or when balance cannot cover all missing essentials, actual missing access-path cells and12 ZC supplies. A funded intact starter does not advertise unneeded aid. Review pauses actual simulation; cancel/Escape do not spend, place or consume a command. Actual paid/refund APIs remain authoritative whenever affordable.

No recovery action grants currency. When missing basic hardware is unaffordable, support supplies only the actual missing habitat, feeder, water station, perch or required path cell, with **no paid-build proof and zero sale/refund value**. Repeated removal/replacement cannot harvest coins. Real graph planning finds a legal site and gate-to-habitat route. Completely paved land offers one safe tile reclaim with the existing actual paid refund and exact Undo repayment; it never overwrites occupied terrain. Unreachable care objects offer an explicit safe repair/removal step. Welcoming two birds and opening the gate require the actual constructed habitat/access; no synthetic ticket income is created.

At balance below12, a present bird's hunger or thirst at least60 and its empty matching portion can enable rescue rations. Each batch tops existing food/water portions up to2 per bird, preserves any larger existing stock, and is limited to once per300 fixed ticks (30 simulation seconds). It cannot be stockpiled above the small base through repeat clicks. Removing/rebuilding bowls, retry/remount and codec handoff do not reset the cooldown. This renewable survival floor deliberately keeps care recoverable while ordinary affordable full refills still cost8/4 ZC and healthy real visitors fund ongoing care. These are game balancing rules, not animal-science facts. No finite one-off coin gift that later strands the player is used.

Successful support consumes the durable quoted command; stale, paused, cancelled or rejected actions leave whole authoritative state unchanged. Serializable recovery metadata records equipment count, last ration tick/batch count and actual gift-habitat identity/build tick. Maintenance covers genuine new/restored habitat periods without forging paid build costs or erasing existing arrears. Full codec validation rejects malformed proof. The earlier bounded-journal/checkpoint solution remains in place; no short-session transaction freeze is reintroduced.
