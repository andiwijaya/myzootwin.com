| Rule | Actual behavior |
|---|---|
| Intake | Manual Open/Close. Real land/path entrance and at least one connected neighbor; no habitat-attraction claim yet. |
| Bounded lifecycle | Capacity 3. While continuously open, a timer of 30 simulation ticks controls spawning, subject to path access and the cap. Lifetime 120 ticks; approach 20, greeting until 80, departure until 120. Slots remain on the real entrance path tile, clear of columns/roof and one another. |
| Identity/state | Monotonic `arrival-N`, per-person serial/slot/spawnTick, page-session memento, strict serializable validation. No synthetic ticket/admission or measured satisfaction. |
| Renderer | Five rig meshes plus a contact shade per person. Shared eight geometry/two material pool, zero texture bytes, released on teardown. Cosmetic walk can be reduced without disabling logic. |
| Ownership | Existing single RAF/tick clock and unsubscribe/dispose contracts; stale renderer handles cannot modify the replacement. |
