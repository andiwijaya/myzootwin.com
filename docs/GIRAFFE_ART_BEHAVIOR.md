Browse is core dietary care and can be presented at different heights. Game consequence: two essential paid browse stations, measured stock consumption, individual water access, shade and recovery, separate optional enrichment. Aesthetic neck movement never consumes stock.
- [Wark & Cronin, 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40440350/): multi-institutional observations connect environment/management with browsing, rumination and activity. Public feeding findings support the PRD's exclusion of guest feeding. Game inference: independent needs, choice of foraging location and loose proximity, rather than a rigid following chain. No claim that arbitrary game welfare weights reproduce empirical animal welfare.
- [Rose, 2023](https://www.mdpi.com/2673-5636/4/3/39): species-specific care includes leaves, rumination, movement and opportunities for every individual. Game inference: browse nutrition is essential, enrichment is optional, all three must access real ports within bounded simulation time. The direct page initially returned429; primary publisher search content subsequently supplied the article and bibliographic details.

No flight, roar, elephant spray, breeding, fighting or calf logic. Reduced motion affects visual amplitude, never simulation outcomes.
